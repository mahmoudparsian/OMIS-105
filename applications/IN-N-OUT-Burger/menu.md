The In-N-Out Menu features a famously simple, straightforward 
list consisting of burgers, fries, drinks, and milkshakes.

Burgers
Double-Double: Two beef patties, two slices of American cheese, 
               lettuce, tomato, spread (Thousand Island), and 
               optional onions. $6.50

Cheeseburger: Single beef patty, one slice of American cheese, 
              lettuce, tomato, spread, and optional onions. $4.55

Hamburger: Single beef patty, lettuce, tomato, spread, and optional onions. $4.00

Note: Number 1 (Double-Double), 2 (Cheeseburger), and 3 (Cheeseburger) 
combos come with French Fries and a medium Drink.

Sides & Beverages

French Fries: Freshly made, 100% real potatoes. $2.50

Beverages: Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, 7Up, Root Beer, Dr Pepper, Minute Maid,
           Pink Lemonade, Iced Tea 
           small: $2.40, medium: $2.55, large: $2.75, X-large: 2.95
Beverages: Coffee: $1.65, and Milk: $1.25

Shakes: Chocolate, Strawberry, or Vanilla: $3.25

The "Not So" Secret Menu In-N-Out is famous for its Not So Secret Menu, 
which features customizations you can ask to add to your order.

Animal Style Burger: Beef patties are mustard-fried, and the burger is 
                     topped with extra spread, pickles, and grilled onions.

Animal Style Fries: French fries topped with a slice of melted cheese, 
                    grilled onions, and spread.

Protein Style: Replaces the bun with crisp lettuce leaves 
               (great for low-carb diets).

Flying Dutchman: Two slices of cheese melted between two beef patties 
                 (no bun, no lettuce, no tomato).

Grilled Cheese: Two slices of melted American cheese, lettuce, tomato, 
                and spread on a toasted bun.

Custom Patties & Cheese: You can order any size burger from a 3x3 
                         (three patties, three cheese) to a 4x4.
