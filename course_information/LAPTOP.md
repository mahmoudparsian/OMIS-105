# Laptop Requirement

## You Must Bring a Fully Charged Laptop to Every Class

Each class session includes an **in-class lab** (see
[`LAB-IN-CLASS-POLICY.md`](./LAB-IN-CLASS-POLICY.md)). Labs are
completed and submitted **during class**, so you need a working
laptop with you every session.

## ☑️ Requirements

* Bring your laptop to **every** class session — fully charged.
* Bring your charger too, in case an outlet is available.
* Install all required software **before** class (see
  [`software_installation/README.md`](../software_installation/README.md)).
* Confirm Python, DuckDB, and Marimo run correctly on your laptop
  before the first lab.

## ⚠️ No Laptop, No Lab

Labs cannot be made up outside of class (see
[`CLASS_ATTENDANCE.md`](./CLASS_ATTENDANCE.md)). If you arrive
without a charged, working laptop, you will not be able to complete
that day's lab, and you will receive a zero (`0`) on that lab —
worth `30 points`.

A dead battery, a forgotten charger, or software installed
incorrectly is **not** an excused reason to miss a lab.

## 💡 Recommendations

* Charge your laptop the night before class.
* Test your setup (Python, DuckDB, Marimo, qStudio) a few days
  before the first lab — not the morning of.
* Keep a backup copy of your work saved outside your laptop
  (e.g., cloud storage or a USB drive).
